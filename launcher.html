Add "To-Do" to your home screen

Your app link is saved ✓

1. Remove the old grey "G" icon (if you have one): press and hold it → Remove App / Remove.
2. Add this page:
  • iPhone (Safari): Share button (square with arrow) → Add to Home Screen → keep the name To-Do → Add.

The new red ✓ icon opens your To-Do straight away. Not in Safari or Chrome? Copy this page's address and open it there first.
